Ten SteelEnv tasks in six failure modes. In each, the agent reads a real shop drawing and builds the fabricated piece as a CadQuery solid. A verifier compares that solid with the piece the fabricator actually cut, within 1.6 mm.

The ten fall into six failure modes: the fabricator's 1/2 in corner convention (3 tasks), the hand of an angle from a single elevation (2), note-only flange cuts and their convention (2), the datum of a dimension chain on a giant sheet (1), a callout attributed to the wrong stacked plate (1), and a bent plate that must be unfolded to its flat blank (1). Drawings are provided as raster images, so the task measures reading the print.

  • Harbor · raster: 17 of 120 attempts solved their task (pass@3, three attempts per model per task). 4 tasks every model fails, 3 only Gemini 4 Argon solves, 3 are solvable by several models. Solved / attempts: Gemini 10/30, Claude 4/30, Astra 3/30, Qwen 0/30.

04

How the verifier works

A strict geometric comparison with the piece the shop actually cut.

Where the answer comes from

Each piece was cut on a CNC line from the fabricator's DSTV NC1 file: the section, the length, the outline contours (copes, cuts and their corner radii) and every hole with its diameter, position and face. That file is parsed into tests/truth.json, the key. The reference solution in solution/ builds the key's solid and scores 1.0; that solid is also shipped as task-NNN/reference.step (with a render, reference.png): it is exactly the part the verifier compares against.

What the model submits

One STEP file at /app/part.step. For the cut-list task, one solid per piece in the same file.

How it is graded

tests/grade.py first aligns the submission to the key by a rigid motion: placement and axis choice are free, but a mirror image is a different part and is not accepted. Then 6 gates: it builds, it is a single solid, it is connected, the B-rep is valid, holes the key drills through are not blind, and it has the key's features (holes). Any failed gate scores 0. Then 7 checks:

CheckHowTolerance
Thicknessplate, leg, web and flange thickness by ray casts through the part±0.5 mm
Overall sizeextents after alignment±1.6 mm
Outline shapecross-sections compared with the key's contours (Hausdorff distance)1.6 mm
Hole countnumber of holesexact
Hole diametersevery matched hole±0.2 mm
Hole positionsoptimal one-to-one matching of holes per face±1.6 mm
Volumerelative volume±3 %

1.6 mm is 1/16 in, the precision shop drawings are dimensioned to. The score is the fraction of checks passed; a task counts as solved only at 1.0. For the cut list, every submitted solid is matched to a key piece and graded the same way; the score is the matched pieces' total over the larger of the key's and the submission's piece count, so a missing or extra piece costs score and the task is solved only when every piece is exact.

Why we trust it

  • The reference solution scores 1.0 on every task, and Harbor's do-nothing agent scores 0.
  • Copies of the reference carrying exactly the models' errors (mirror image, square corner, slot instead of cut-back, stations shifted by the base plate, wrong hole size) all fail.
  • Empty, box-shaped and other cheat parts score 0 (one featureless bar of the cut list aside; section 8).
  • An independent audit compared the verifier's verdict with the drawing on 40 real model parts: 35 agreed, 4 hinged on details the drawing leaves open, 1 traced to an error in that task's answer key rather than in the verifier; no part was passed that should have failed.

The benchmark

Each task takes a real, verified fabrication model of a steel structure, removes the pieces detailed on one or more shop drawings, and gives the agent what a detailer would have: the partial model, the shop drawings of the missing pieces, the erection drawings of the area and the section outlines. The agent must rebuild every missing part, in place, by operating the FreeCAD desktop. The 47 tasks are 36 rebuilds, 8 change orders and 3 designs. Drawings, parts and positions all come from fabricated work. Nothing was drawn or modelled for the benchmark, and the title blocks are blanked.

The computer-1 agent in Harbor sees a FreeCAD 1.0.2 desktop at 1280 × 896 only through screenshots and answers each one with a single mouse or keyboard action, for up to 1,500 steps and 6 h per attempt. There is no terminal, FreeCAD's Python console and macros are disabled, and the container has no network. A separate verifier container grades only the saved FreeCAD document and its STEP export.

Why the tasks are hard

Every number has to be read off a raster drawing through screenshots, and every part placed in the plant's own coordinates through dialogs and the property editor. A task asks for 1 to 25 parts (9.1 on average). Grading is strict: a task is solved only at reward 1.0, with every part within 1.6 mm (1/16 in) of the real one, every hole open and no clash with the existing steel. An attempt with 6 of 8 parts right scores 0.675 and still fails. The headline therefore counts solved attempts; pass@3 counts a task as passed when at least 2 of its 3 primary attempts solve it, and pass^3 when all 3 do.

FreeCAD 1.0.2 runs on the desktop (X display `:1`, 1280 x 896). This task is for screen-only agents: do all the
modelling by operating FreeCAD's graphical interface with the mouse and keyboard: import, sketches, pads, pockets,
primitives, extrusions, booleans, placements and so on. FreeCAD's Python console and macro commands are disabled; do
not script FreeCAD in any way, do not start other programs to make geometry, and do not write CAD files by any means
other than FreeCAD's own Save and Export dialogs. There is no network access.

Useful GUI tools: File > Import (STEP) loads the region and the profile outlines; the property editor shows a
selected object's Placement and lets you type exact positions and rotations; the status bar shows the coordinates of
the point under the cursor; Part > Primitives, Part > Extrude and Part > Boolean > Cut make prisms and holes; the
drawing images can be opened inside FreeCAD with Tools > Load image.

## What to save

1. Save the FreeCAD document as **`/app/output/rebuild.FCStd`**.
2. Export the parts you added as STEP to **`/app/output/full.step`** (select them, File > Export, STEP), one solid per
   part, in the model's coordinates. Exporting the whole model (the region plus your parts) is also accepted.
   Labelling your objects with the piece marks is recommended, not graded.
3. The FEM analysis with its result and the `Results` spreadsheet are part of the saved document `rebuild.FCStd`.

## How it is graded

The grader reads your two files; it does not run anything you wrote. Every given part must still be in your saved
document, unmoved, and every solid you export must also be in the saved document. Each missing part is paired with
the exported solid that overlaps it most in place (model coordinates, no re-alignment) and checked against the real
part:

- **shape and place**: its surface must lie within 1.6 mm (1/16 in) of the real part's surface everywhere - length,
  section, end cuts, copes, clips and outline included (the root-fillet and corner-radius zones of rolled sections
  are not checked, so a section drawn with sharp corners is fine);
- **holes**: every hole of the real part must be open in yours, with its diameter within 0.8 mm of the real one;
- a part may not overlap the existing steel by more than 1 cm3 beyond what the real part does.
- **the design**: the FEM analysis in your saved document - material (E and Poisson's ratio), the fixed faces and the loaded faces (they must be the stated faces of your beam), a total force of 60 000 N pointing down, a mesh of your beam, the CalculiX solver and a result whose maximum displacement is within 15 % of the reference for the right section; and the `Results` spreadsheet with that result (within 3 %) and the chosen section. The beam itself is graded like every other part, so it must have the right section.

No bolts are graded. Full credit (1.0) needs every check passed, every exported solid paired with a missing part and
the given model unchanged; otherwise the score is 0.9 x (0.6 x parts right / missing parts + 0.25 x FEM checks passed / FEM checks + 0.15 x recorded-result checks passed / recorded-result checks) x (paired solids / exported solids).
A missing or unreadable document, a document not saved from the GUI, or a changed given model scores 0.
